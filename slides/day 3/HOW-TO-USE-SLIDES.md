## Option A: Opening in Microsoft PowerPoint or Google Slides
- **Microsoft PowerPoint:** Simply double-click `Day-3-Part-1-Angular-SPA.pptx` or `Day-3-Part-2-DevTools-and-Bug-Reporting.pptx`. Speaker notes are already embedded inside the Notes pane of every slide!
- **Google Slides:** Upload the `.pptx` files to Google Drive and open with Google Slides—formatting, tables, diagrams, and speaker notes are preserved.

## Option B: Zero-Install Interactive HTML Viewer (Recommended Backup!)
Double-click either `.html` file to open it directly in **Google Chrome**:
- Press **`F11`** for full-screen projector mode.
- Use **`←` / `→`** arrow keys (or on-screen buttons) to move between slides.
- Press **`N`** to toggle **Instructor Speaker Notes** on/off!
