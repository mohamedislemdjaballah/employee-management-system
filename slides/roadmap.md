## Slide 4: What to Expect
- **Big-Picture Overview, Not Deep Coding:** Our goal is to give you a clear overview of every tool and framework in the stack—what it does, why we use it, and how the pieces talk to each other—without getting bogged down in deep technical details.
- **Interrupt & Ask Questions Anytime:** Please don't wait until the end of a slide or session! If a term, acronym, or concept isn't clear, feel free to jump in and ask right away.
- **No Question Is Too Basic:** This workshop is designed to demystify modern web terminology and make it approachable for everyone.
- **Summary of Topics & Tools We Will Cover:**
  - **Web Architecture & APIs:** Monolithic vs. Decoupled vs. Microservices, HTTP & REST APIs
  - **Backend:** Java & Spring Boot
  - **Database:** PostgreSQL, Data Modeling & pgAdmin
  - **Frontend:** Single-Page Applications (SPAs) & Angular
  - **DevOps & Infrastructure:** Git, Docker & Nginx (Web Server & Reverse Proxy)
  - **Security:** Authentication & Authorization
